The cast so far

Every person page leads into the exact panel they appear in.

Cao Cao曹操
155–220
Chancellor of Han and master of the north. Poet, administrator, and the man the novel made its villain.
Liu Bei劉備
161–223
A claimant with a famous name and almost nothing else. Began 208 in flight; ended it holding the south of Jing.
Zhou Yu周瑜
175–210
Chief commander of Wu. Read Cao Cao's army correctly and burned it. Died of illness in 210, aged 36.
Zhuge Liang諸葛亮
181–234
Liu Bei's strategist, 27 years old in 208. Negotiated the alliance; later governed Shu-Han.
Yuan Shao袁紹
d. 202
Heir of the Yuan clan of Runan, four generations of senior ministers. Master of four provinces north of the Yellow River — until Guandu.
Guan Yu關羽
d. 220
A general of Liu Bei from the first raising of troops. In 200, briefly in Cao Cao's camp, he ended Yan Liang at Boma — then went back.

Why trust a comic?

Sourced to the scroll. Every caption and quotation carries its book and chapter. No "it is said".
The novel stays out. Romance of the Three Kingdoms is literature, not evidence. It appears only in Myth Checks.
Disagreement shown. Numbers, sites and motives the scholars argue about are tagged Debated, not smoothed over.
