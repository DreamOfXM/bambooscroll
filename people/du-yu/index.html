Du Yu 杜預
222–285 · Three Kingdoms

Grand general of the south who took Jiangling and said the war would open like split bamboo. A soldier with a scholar's library — the age called him the Armory.

Our momentum is like splitting bamboo — after a few joints, the rest parts to the blade, and there is nothing left to resist.Du Yu to his generals who counselled waiting, 280; Jin shu, ch. 34, Biography of Du Yu

Myth vs record

The story you may know

A plain battlefield commander remembered only for one proverb.

What the sources say

The age called him the Armory — a soldier with a scholar's library. He succeeded Yang Hu as the southern commander and kept the upstream plan alive at Xiangyang; the split-bamboo image was his own argument to impatient generals, spoken before Jiangling fell, and it became the proverb of the campaign.
