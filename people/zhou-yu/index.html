Zhou Yu 周瑜
175–210 · Three Kingdoms

Chief commander of Wu. Read Cao Cao's army correctly and burned it. Died of illness in 210, aged 36.

Being with Zhou Yu is like drinking fine wine — you are drunk before you know it.Cheng Pu, quoted in the Jiangbiao zhuan, in Pei Songzhi's commentary

Myth vs record

The story you may know

A jealous rival of Zhuge Liang, outwitted three times and dead of rage.

What the sources say

He died of illness in 210, two years before the events the novel stages between them. His own colleagues described a man of such warmth that men forgot themselves in his company.
