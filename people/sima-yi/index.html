Sima Yi 司馬懿
179–251 · Three Kingdoms

Wei's commander in the west from 231. Refused battle at the Wuzhang Plains, outlived Zhuge Liang, and left his house the empire.

I can judge the living. I cannot judge the dead.On the saying that the dead Zhuge Liang routed him; Han Jin Chunqiu, in Pei Songzhi's commentary

Relations

  • Zhuge Liang opponents — Four years of refused battle on the Wei river. He outlived him, and that decided it.
  • Cao Cao reluctant servant — Cao Cao distrusted him and kept him anyway; he outlived the whole house and took what it held.

Myth vs record

The story you may know

The white-faced villain of opera, outwitted at every turn and frightened off by an empty city.

What the sources say

The histories give the colder read: he refused battle because refusing was correct, asked the court for a permission he knew would be denied, and measured his enemy by his appetite. The empty city is a story Pei Songzhi quotes in order to disprove.
