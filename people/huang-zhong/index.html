Huang Zhong 黃忠
d. 220 · Three Kingdoms

Liu Bei's veteran general. Charged down Mount Dingjun with the drums rolling in 219, broke Xiahou Yuan's army, and slew him.

He pushed his vanguard forward with the drums rolling; Yuan's army was badly defeated, and Yuan was beheaded.Sanguozhi, Shu shu 6, Biography of Huang Zhong

Relations

  • Liu Bei lord & general — For that one day at Dingjun he was raised to General Who Attacks the West.

Myth vs record

The story you may know

He slew Xiahou Yuan in single combat, as in the opera Dingjun Mountain.

What the sources say

No duel is recorded. The histories give an assault: he charged down from the heights with the drums rolling, Yuan's army broke on the slope, and Yuan died in the rout together with Zhao Yong. For that one day he was raised to General Who Attacks the West.
