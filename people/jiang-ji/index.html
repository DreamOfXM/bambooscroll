Jiang Ji 蔣濟
d. 249 CE · Three Kingdoms

Veteran minister whose name gave weight to an oath. His letter to the camp promised Cao Shuang no more than the loss of office; he watched the promise break, and died of remorse within months.

His letter went into the camp promising the regent no more than the loss of office; when the promise was broken, he died of remorse within months.Sanguozhi, Wei shu 15, Biography of Jiang Ji; Zizhi Tongjian, scroll 75

Myth vs record

The story you may know

A willing architect of Sima Yi's coup who profited from the broken oath.

What the sources say

He and Chen Tai carried the promise of safety to the camp; after the executions he asked Sima Yi to keep faith, could not prevail, and died of remorse within months — the coup's quietest casualty.
