Myth vs record

The story you may know

The man who wrote the New dynasty's rituals went down loyally with it, a scholar's martyr to the throne that had taken his own clan's empire.

What the sources say

He served that throne for over a decade and then plotted against it, and the record preserves both halves without stitching them into a motive. An old general had already asked publicly for his head, naming him as the one who had inverted the Five Classics and made the scholars unsure; Mang was furious, had the man led out, and then took part of what he said. When the scholar's own plot failed in 23 CE he killed himself with a general of the guard, and the book's point about the cover-up is not about loyalty but about appearances: the emperor could not bear the scandal of an inner quarrel, so he hid how they died.
