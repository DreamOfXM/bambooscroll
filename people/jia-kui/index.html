Jia Kui 賈逵
d. 228 · Three Kingdoms

Governor of Yuzhou on Wei's eastern flank. Warned, marched anyway, arrived too late for the battle — and just in time to save Cao Xiu.

He had warned against trusting the defector and against the deep advance; he marched anyway, and his fresh column at Jiakou saved the remnant of Cao Xiu's army.Sanguozhi, Wei shu 15, Biography of Jia Kui; Zizhi Tongjian, scroll 71

Myth vs record

The story you may know

A willing architect of Wei's deep advance into Wu.

What the sources say

The Wei records keep his warning — that the deep advance was dangerous and that Zhou Fang's defection should not be trusted — and his final counsel on his deathbed that the east be held by steady defence rather than great offensives.
