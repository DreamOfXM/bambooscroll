Liu Bei 劉備
161–223 · Three Kingdoms

A claimant with a famous name and almost nothing else. Began 208 in flight; ended it holding the south of Jing.

Resolute and generous, wide in heart and courteous to men — he had the wind of the First Emperor of Han, and the make of a hero.Chen Shou's appraisal, Sanguozhi, Shu shu 2

Relations

  • Zhuge Liang lord & strategist — Three visits to a thatched cottage — the visits are in the sources; the number three is in the memorial.
  • Cao Cao rivals — The two halves of the age.
  • Guan Yu lord & general — From the first raising of troops to the last. The histories say they shared a bed and a table, as brothers.

Myth vs record

The story you may know

A weeping weakling who won by kindness alone and let others fight for him.

What the sources say

The histories record a frontline commander from 184 to 223 who led his own campaigns; at Changban he chose to lose his army's speed rather than abandon the people following him.
