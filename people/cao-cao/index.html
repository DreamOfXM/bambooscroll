Cao Cao 曹操
155–220 · Three Kingdoms

Chancellor of Han and master of the north. Poet, administrator, and the man the novel made its villain.

A man beyond the ordinary, a hero above his age.Chen Shou's appraisal, Sanguozhi, Wei shu 1

Relations

  • Liu Bei rivals — Once hosted him, twice fought him, never finished him.
  • Zhou Yu opponents — Red Cliff, 208 CE.
  • Yuan Shao rivals — Friends in their youth at Luoyang; Guandu decided which of them the north would keep.
  • Guan Yu brief master — Held him in 200, honoured him, and let him go.

Myth vs record

The story you may know

The white-faced villain of opera and novel, cruel and treacherous by nature.

What the sources say

The official history appraises him as the ablest man of his age; his own annals record the Red Cliff defeat in one flat sentence, without blaming anyone.
